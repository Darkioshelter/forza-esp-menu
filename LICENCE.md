## 🧩 About

**Forza Horizon overlay Menu** is a curated list of Forza Horizon open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, minimap tweaks, menu configurations
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — car catalog, performance notes, overlay configurations
- **Research Notes** — race layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Driving Helpers
- Speedometer Overlay — real-time speed display
- Minimap Enhancer — improved map visibility
- Route Notes — record and review race lines
- Playback Speed — adjust replay speed for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Car Catalog — browse cars, colors, and upgrades
- Custom Livery Notes — organize player designs
- Visual Tuning — improved car visibility

### 🏋️ Race Layout
- Race Mode Notes — segment study on any track
- Camera Control — manually adjust camera angles
- Jump Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the editor
